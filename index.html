Land cover change in Kampala, 2005–2026

Kampala District, 2026

Four epochs of satellite imagery classified into lake, urban areas, wetlands, tree–grass mixture and urban grassland. I segmented each scene with mean-shift segmentation, classified the segments with a support vector machine, and checked each map against independent reference points (overall accuracy 72–88 %, kappa 0.63–0.83).

  • ArcGIS Pro
  • Landsat 8
  • Sentinel-2
  • Object-based classification
  • Accuracy assessment

HYDRO_SYNC: digital field data for hydrogeology

Tethys Geoscience Mentorship Program, 2026

An open-source web platform that replaces paper field sheets for groundwater work. Field teams record VES and resistivity surveys and site data on digital forms with GPS capture; records sync to a central groundwater database and appear on a live map for analysis and reporting.

  • Python
  • Tethys Platform
  • Web mapping
  • GPS capture
